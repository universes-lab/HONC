Rules drawn

  1. Each ring passes through the point hole of its torus along the torus axis.
  2. Tori never overlap; neighbouring tori touch.
  3. Torus surface area equals the sphere's, 4πR². Round tube: a = b = R/√π, outer radius 1.128 R.
  4. Ring length equals the equator, 2πR. Ring shape: ellipse.
  5. Two rings sharing a hole move together there.
  6. Quantron torus: its ring lies on its equator, so its outer radius is R and the tube is stretched along the axis, b_z = 0.760 R.
